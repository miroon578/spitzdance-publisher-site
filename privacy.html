Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpitzDance Publisher handles information when users operate the desktop application.

1. Information We Handle

The application may process video files, titles, descriptions, scheduled publication times, platform settings, and account authorization information necessary to provide its features.

2. Video Files

Video files are selected by the user from their own device. When a user initiates publication, the selected content is sent to the relevant social media platform through its supported API.

3. Account Authorization

When a user connects a social media account, the application uses the authorization process provided by that platform. Access tokens and authorization files may be stored locally on the user's computer. Users should protect their device and must not share these files.

4. Third-Party Platforms

When content is published through YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or another supported service, that platform processes information according to its own privacy policy and settings.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell users' personal information. Information necessary to publish content is transmitted to the platform selected by the user.

6. Data Retention and Security

Application settings, publication queues, logs, and authorization information may remain on the user's computer until deleted. Users should secure their device and remove stored credentials when they no longer need them.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using the application, disconnect platform access through the relevant platform's account settings, and delete local application data. Deleting local data may affect scheduled jobs and saved authorization.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to be used by children in violation of applicable laws or the requirements of connected platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: spitzdance@gmail.com
